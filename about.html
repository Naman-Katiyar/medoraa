About Medora

Medora turns a branded medicine name into the two things a patient needs to know: what molecule it is, and what it should cost.

Medora is a free, open-source Progressive Web App. It works fully offline, needs no sign-up, and is built for a cheap phone on a bad signal.

Data Sources

  • PM Jan Aushadhi (PMBI)Published product list and prices - the generic prices in this app
  • NPPANational Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority ceiling prices

Gemma 4 Photo Reading (Optional)

Medora can send a photo of a printed medicine strip to a Gemma 4 vision model (Google AI Studio) to read the brand name.

Gemma settings saved on this device.

Disclaimer: Medora shows price and molecule information only. It is not medical advice.
